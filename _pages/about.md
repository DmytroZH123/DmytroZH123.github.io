Hello everyone! I am an Applied Data Scientist, Artificial Intelligence Engineer, and currently pursuing a Master's degree in Data Science at the [University of Vienna](https://www.univie.ac.at/). I graduated from [Kyiv National Economic University](https://kneu.edu.ua/) with a Bachelor's degree in System Analysis with distinction.

I have worked at the fast-growing cybersecurity company [Whalebone](https://www.whalebone.io/), which develops zero-disruption products for Telcos, ISPs, and enterprises, as a Threat Intelligence Specialist in Brno, Czech Republic. 
At present, I am working at [SBA Research](https://www.sba-research.org/), Austria's largest research center dedicated exclusively to information security, as an R&D Cybersecurity Analyst in Vienna, Austria, alongside my studies.

During my Master's studies, I was a Visiting Summer Researcher at [CERN](https://home.cern/) , where I worked on full-text retrieval for the full-text screening stage of systematic reviews within the [NeutrinoReview project](https://neutrino-review.web.cern.ch/). I was supervised by [Elias Sandner](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=moQ6B8IAAAAJ&hl=en) (CERN) and [Christian Guetl](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=NoAiKpcAAAAJ&hl=de) from the [Cognitive and Digital Science Lab](https://www.tugraz.at/institute/hcc/research/research-groups/cognitive-digital-science-lab/codis) (Technical University of Graz). Research output: 1 peer-reviewed article presented at a conference. I was also a Visiting Winter Researcher at [CISPA](https://cispa.de/en) and a fellow in the [Interdisciplinary Research Incubator for AI Safety](https://www.ais-saarland.org/interdisciplinary-research-incubator), where I collaborated with the [SprintML Group](https://sprintml.com/) on fine-tuning open-source large language models for the identification of training datasets in generative AI. Research output: a research task submitted to the CISPA European Cybersecurity & AI Hackathon Championship. At [Imperial College London](https://www.imperial.ac.uk/), I worked with the [Security & Machine Learning Lab](https://sml.cybersec.fun/) surpevised by [Sergio Maffeis](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=ODKP7a0AAAAJ&hl=en) and [Fahad Alotaibi](https://orcid.org/0000-0003-2901-5313), on reliable and auditable malware dataset labelling for ML research.Research output: 1 peer-reviewed article presented at a conference. Moreover, at the [University of Cambridge](https://www.cam.ac.uk/) and [Nokia Bell Labs](https://www.nokia.com/bell-labs/about/locations/cambridge-uk/), I worked with the [HIVE CAI Lav](https://hive.chia.cam.ac.uk/) under [Malak Sadek](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=-0Sh_uAAAAAJ&hl=en) and [Edyta Bogucka](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=EdAme-MAAAAJ&hl) on value-shape visualization in language models. Research output: 2 peer-reviewed articles at a conference. 

My research interests include but are not limited to: *Human-Computer Interaction, Explainable and Transparent Artificial Intelligence, Natural Language Processing, Random Processes, and Reinforcement Learning*.

Communicative, responsible, problem-solving, teamwork, creative, have great attention to details, able to work both in team and independently. I have analytical thinking, persistent in achieving goals, and optimist myself. Also, I like jazz music, opera, Italian art, and travelling.
